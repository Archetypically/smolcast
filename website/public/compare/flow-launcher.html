Pick the one that fits

Go with Flow Launcher if

  • You want plugins, or to write your own
  • You use Everything and search inside files
  • You want themes and browser bookmarks
  • You’re on Windows 10

Get Flow Launcher

Go with smolcast if

  • You want clipboard, emoji and snippets out of the box
  • You want unit conversions that paste on Enter
  • You want one small .exe and nothing else
  • You’d rather not manage plugins
