Pick the one that fits

Go with smolcast if

  • You just want the essentials, fast
  • You’d rather not make an account
  • You like a small app you can read the code of
  • You want zero AI and zero telemetry

Go with Raycast if

  • You want extensions (Linear, GitHub, Notion, Spotify…)
  • You want AI right in the launcher
  • You need window management
  • You also use Raycast on a Mac
  • You’re on Windows 10
