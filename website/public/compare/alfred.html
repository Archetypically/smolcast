Pick the one that fits

Go with smolcast if

  • You’re on Windows 11
  • You want clipboard history and snippets free
  • You want an open-source launcher
  • You want something tiny

Go with Alfred if

  • You’re on a Mac (smolcast isn’t)
  • You want workflows
  • You want snippets that expand as you type
